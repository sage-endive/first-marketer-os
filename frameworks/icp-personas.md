## Purpose

This framework defines your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) and the individual buyer personas within it. It is the foundation everything else in this system builds on — positioning, messaging, campaign targeting, and sales enablement all derive from a clear ICP.

**ICP** = the type of company most likely to buy, get value, and renew/expand.
**Persona** = the individual within that company who buys, uses, or influences the purchase.

They are different. Both matter.
